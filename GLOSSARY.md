# Aperture

A companion experience that adds a launch ritual and optional controls around the user's Codex desktop app.

## Language

**Companion**:
The separate experience surrounding Codex, with its own controls and lifetime.
_Avoid_: Codex modification, replacement client

**Aperture**:
The six-vane geometric mark shared by the opening sequence and companion controls.
_Avoid_: AI orb, spinner

**Launch ritual**:
The brief opening sequence accompanying an explicit request to open Codex.
_Avoid_: loading progress, model initialization

**Tab**:
The small, stationary companion control available while the target app is active.

**Deck**:
The expanded companion controls opened from the tab or menu bar.

**Quiet mode**:
The preference that hides the tab and suppresses decorative motion while preserving menu-bar access.

**Target app**:
The installed application the user selects to open Codex; it can contain other experiences besides Codex.
